# Halloween trends Dashboard
 Project link: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/oviya.s5224/viz/HalloweenAssignment_17881128418780/Dashboard2?publish=yes
 The **Halloween Trends Dashboard** is a festive, themed visualization that pulls together multiple perspectives on visitor turnout during Halloween events. 

### 🎃 Core Metrics
- **Total Visitors:** 4,147 — the overall attendance captured across the dataset.  
- **Average Visitors per Year:** 592.4 — gives a sense of yearly turnout trends.

### ⏰ Time-Based Insights
- **Turnout Curve by Time:** Shows how visitor counts fluctuate between 6:00 pm and 8:15 pm. This helps identify peak trick‑or‑treating hours.  
- **Day-of-Week Overlay:** Compares turnout across weekdays, highlighting which days Halloween celebrations drew the most visitors.

### 📅 Yearly & Seasonal Patterns
- **Year-wise Traffic (2011 focus):** A bar chart zooms into one year’s traffic by time, useful for spotting anomalies or standout evenings.  
- **Weather-driven Dips:** Displays years (2009, 2011, 2013, 2015) where turnout dropped, likely due to poor weather conditions. This adds context to fluctuations.

### 📊 Visitor Segmentation
- **Visitor Strength Tier:** Categorizes turnout into “Low,” “Average,” and “High.” Notably, 2010 and 2011 are marked as high‑traffic years.  
- **Forecast:** A line graph projecting visitor counts from 2008–2015, showing long‑term trends and expected future turnout.

### 👀 Design & Theme
- Purple background with orange/yellow highlights creates a Halloween vibe.  
- Playful yet structured layout makes the dashboard engaging while still informative.

---

✨ **Overall Takeaway:**  
This dashboard doesn’t just show numbers — it tells a story of Halloween participation over time, across days, and under different conditions. It’s a strong example of how themed dashboards can combine **data storytelling** with **visual appeal**.
